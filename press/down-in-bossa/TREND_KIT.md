# #BossaFreeze: Down In Bossa trend kit
**ACND × Ash Army · Down In Bossa · Profi Music · out now (30.10.2026)**

## The trend in one line
**When the beat stops, you freeze. When it drops, you go mirror.**

At **0:45.4** the kick and bass in Down In Bossa cut out for exactly one bar, about 2 seconds of just the airy top end. Then the full beat slams back in at **0:47.4**. That gap is the trend:
- You dance.
- The beat cuts out.
- You hold perfectly still, mid-move.
- The drop hits, and the frame explodes into the ACND mirror kaleidoscope.

Anyone can do it, alone or with friends, and it reads in under 3 seconds. Every copy carries the song, because the freeze only works on this track.

## The one setting that makes or breaks the trend
- **Post with the official sound.** On TikTok and Instagram, add the Profi Music release from the in-app music library. Don't rely on the audio baked into the file: mute the clip's own audio when you add it.
  - Every view, remix and copy then counts toward the song's sound page. That page is what the algorithm pushes, and what people tap to join in.
- **Start the sound at 0:39.6.** All the #BossaFreeze reels are cut to 0:39.6–0:55.3 (8 bars), so the freeze lands 5.9 s into every video.
  - Ask Profi Music, or use TikTok for Artists, to set the song's preview clip to start at 0:39.6. Everyone who taps the sound then gets the freeze at the same moment.
- **Fallback:** the MP4s carry the track (AAC 320k) for platforms without the sound, such as Shorts.

## Rights and safety notes
- **Third-party footage:** the festival and dancer clips came from the Drive packs. They are Pinterest re-uploads of other people's videos.
  - Clips with a visible watermark or creator handle were left out.
  - The rest can still get copyright claims or takedowns, and some show identifiable people.
  - The more of your own footage the trend runs on, the safer it is. That means the D3 freeze clip and Reel 15, the background template, which uses only crowd and light footage behind a dark centre.
- **Credits:** captions only name ACND, Ash Army and Profi Music. No other artists are named or tagged.
